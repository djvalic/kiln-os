# Deviations from stock: quick reference

Everything the open stack does differently from the stock AD5X (software 3.1.0, control 1.1.1),
for when something misbehaves on the real printer. Each entry gives the symptom to look for and how
to go back to stock behaviour. Keep this list current: add an entry whenever a change from stock is
made.

Risk: **High** = could damage hardware or stop a print, **Med** = could stop startup, a swap or a
feature, **Low** = cosmetic or easy to spot.

Last updated: 2026-10-27 (issue #17: the board fan watch is a reactor timer, `[pcb_fan_watch]`; X/Y run currents set to the community values; homing Y back-off keeps Z; MCU and RAM notes corrected). Kernel changes (eMMC clock, CMA) are held back until a printer test boot.

## Motion MCU firmware (`tools/build_mcu.sh`, `klipper/mcu-configs/`)

| Risk | Change | Stock | Ours | If it bites | Revert |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Low | Eboard hex start-address record | Type-05 record = reset handler (0x08016A7D) | Same convention (fixed 2026-10-15; was the image start 0x08010000 from plain `objcopy`) | Eboard doesn't boot after IAPCommand flashes it | Reflash stock `ADM_App.hex` |
| Med | Klipper version | Upstream June–Sept 2024 (≈ v0.12.0-256) | Mainline v0.13.0-816-gee2c6fb6b | Host and MCU must share one protocol (the data dictionary), not one version string: "MCU Protocol error" if they do not | Flash stock `AD5X.bin` / `ADM_App.hex` and run the stock host |
| Med | RAM size | Initial SP 0x20020000 (128 KB) | 0x20010000 (64 KB, mainline's N32G45x default) | "Move queue overflow" or out-of-memory shutdowns under heavy load | Use the xblax build (SP 0x20020000), or patch the RAM size in mainline's Kconfig |
| Low | Startup pin state | Unknown | `INITIAL_PINS="!PB9"` on the mainboard (bed heater held off until configured; from the AD5M mod) | Bed heater behaves oddly before Klipper connects | Drop `CONFIG_INITIAL_PINS` from `ad5x-mainboard.mainline.config` |
| High | Board firmware updates | Flashforge's `AD5X.bin` / `ADM_App.hex`, written by stock only when its `Update`/`UpdateM` flags exist | `ad5x-mcu boot` (`S60ad5x-eboard`) writes the image's mainline Klipper (`/usr/share/ad5x/mcu`, `klipper/mcu-firmware/`) to any board that reports another version, with Flashforge's own `NationsCommand`/`IAPCommand` copied from stock's control directory on usershare; arms stock's `Update`/`UpdateM` first; creates `klipper.enable` once both match. `docs/mcu-flashing.md` | Screen "Switch off and on" (eboard missed its bootloader window) or "Board update failed": `ad5x-mcu status`, `/data/ad5x/mcu/log`, `iap.log`/`nations.log` there | `/data/ad5x/mcu.disable` (no flashing); back to Flashforge's firmware: boot stock and power-cycle (its armed board update) |
| Low | Removed MCU commands | `set_radar_interval`, `set_radar_sync_pulse`, `Mainboard`/`Exboard close=…` message | Not present | Only matters if something outside Klipper used them (nothing found) | Stock firmware |
| Low | PWM resolution | `PWM_MAX` 255 | 32768 | Fan or LED duty looks different | None needed; the host scales it |
| Low | Compiler | Flashforge's toolchain | Arch arm-none-eabi-gcc 16.2 | Timing-sensitive oddities (unlikely) | Build with the AD5M mod's Buildroot toolchain |
| Low | Eboard firmware restart | Stock firmware: reset skips the bootloader wait (backup register 0x1234) | Mainline plus xblax's two eboard patches (`klipper/mcu-patches/`): same bypass, and a bootloader request becomes a plain reset | `FIRMWARE_RESTART` leaves the eboard in its bootloader (klippy can't reconnect) | Power-cycle; or `boot_eboard` |

## Klipper host (`klipper/ad5x.cfg`, see `docs/klipper-fork-diff.md`)

| Risk | Change | Stock | Ours | If it bites | Revert |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Low | Half-duplex (RS-485) serial keep-alive (`buildroot/package/klipper/0001-serialhdl-...patch`) | Upstream `serialhdl.py` | Only for an MCU that advertises `SERIAL_RS485_TDD` (the IFS on Klipper firmware, opt-in). Sends `debug_nop` after 20 ms of silence, and no sooner than 20 ms after any send or received block | A stalled IFS link if the keep-alive is too frequent for the bus | Remove the patch from the package (the MCU does not advertise the constant without the opt-in) |
| High | TMC driver fault checks | Turned off in the fork's `tmc.py` | On (mainline) | Print stops with a TMC/`DRV_STATUS`/`GSTAT` error that stock ignored | Fix the wiring or driver first. Turning the checks off hides real overtemperature or short faults |
| Med | Extruder min temp | `min_temp: -100` | `0` | "ADC out of range" shutdown on a cold start if the thermistor reads near 0 | Lower `min_temp` slightly; -100 can't catch an unplugged thermistor |
| Med | Cold extrusion | `min_extrude_temp: 0` | `170` | IFS load, unload or the cut retract fails with "Extrude below minimum temp" | Heat first, or lower `min_extrude_temp` |
| Med | Hotend heating check | `[verify_heater extruder] max_error: 290` | `120` (mainline default) | "Heater extruder not heating at expected rate" during fan-on purges | Raise `max_error` toward 290, no higher |
| Med | Heater "settled" test | Fork's `heaters.py`: ±3 °C and 0.9 °C/s | Mainline: ±1 °C and 0.1 °C/s | `M109`/`M190` wait noticeably longer before printing | Override `M109`/`M190` with `TEMPERATURE_WAIT` |
| Med | Homing sync timeout | 50 ms (fork's `mcu.py`) | 25 ms (mainline) | "Communication timeout during homing" on X (endstop on the eboard, steppers on the mainboard) | Apply `klipper/patches/trsync-timeout-50ms.patch` (prepared, not applied) |
| Med | Thread priority | Main thread reniced to -10, serial threads to -20, in code | Whole klippy process at `Nice=-10` (`os/systemd/klipper.service`, draft) | "Timer too close" or stutter on the slow MIPS host | Give the serial threads -20 (a small Klipper patch) |

Checked every second from a reactor timer | Fan never runs, or runs at the wrong times (PA5's purpose unconfirmed) | Edit `[pcb_fan_watch]` in local.cfg, or `SET_FAN_SPEED FAN=pcb_fan SPEED=0` |
| Med | Board fan watch | None: the fan is on only from the print start | `[pcb_fan_watch]` extra (reactor timer). | Med | Homing | `HOME`/`MYG67` macros | `[homing_override]` + `_HOME_ZXY`, same Z→X→Y order and Y back-off (the back-off runs on any Y home, not only the full HOME, and keeps the current Z height) | Homing collides or ends in the wrong order | Compare with the stock macros in `klipper/stock-reference/` |
| Low | Z after homing | Z ends at the homed stop (220, bed fully down) | `_HOME_ZXY` lowers the nozzle to Z 215 after `G28 Z`, leaving room for the swap's +5 mm lift | Bed sits 5 mm higher than before after a home | Remove the `G1 Z215` line from `_HOME_ZXY` in `klipper/ad5x.cfg` |
